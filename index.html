AstroCalc Demo

A browser front end for every endpoint of the AstroCalc API: astronomical and astrological calculations for any date and place, shown as tables with the raw JSON alongside.

  • Longitudes and positions of the Sun, Moon, planets and nodes, tropical or sidereal
  • House cusps in fourteen systems, plus the 36 Gauquelin sectors
  • The ascendant and other chart angles
  • Ayanamshas: the offsets of the sidereal zodiacs
  • Rise, set and transit times, for one day or day by day
  • Retrograde stations and planetary movements over time
  • The solar system: a year of the planets' orbits around the Sun
  • Moon phases, tithis, nakshatras, phenomena and aspects between bodies
  • The Indian day from sunrise to sunrise, in ghatis and vighatis
  • Named fixed stars and transposed positions of historic points
